# Research Project Requirements

## 1. Project Type

Group research project.

The project will investigate research problems at the intersection of:

1. Blockchain and Artificial Intelligence
2. Blockchain and Quantum Computing

The final research direction has NOT yet been selected.

---

# 2. Research Scope

The project must remain within one of the following two domains:

## Domain A — Blockchain × AI

Explore both directions:

* AI applied to Blockchain
* Blockchain applied to AI
* AI and Blockchain as an integrated system

AI should NOT be treated as a single technology.

Relevant AI categories may include:

* Machine Learning
* Deep Learning
* Reinforcement Learning
* Generative AI
* Large Language Models
* Agentic AI
* Multi-Agent Systems
* Graph Neural Networks
* NLP
* Anomaly Detection
* Federated Learning
* Explainable AI
* AI Security
* AI Evaluation
* Optimization
* Other relevant AI approaches discovered during research

Blockchain areas may include:

* Consensus
* Proof-of-Work
* Proof-of-Stake
* Smart Contracts
* Blockchain Security
* Scalability
* Layer-2
* Zero-Knowledge Proofs
* Privacy
* Interoperability
* MEV
* Transaction Ordering
* Decentralized Identity
* Governance
* Blockchain Data
* Decentralized Systems
* Other relevant areas discovered during research

---

## Domain B — Blockchain × Quantum

Explore both directions:

* Quantum Computing applied to Blockchain
* Blockchain applied to Quantum Computing
* Blockchain and Quantum Computing as an integrated system

Quantum areas may include:

* Quantum Algorithms
* Quantum Cryptography
* Post-Quantum Cryptography
* Quantum Key Distribution
* Quantum Networks
* Quantum Security
* Quantum Optimization
* Quantum Computing
* Quantum Error Correction
* Quantum Error Mitigation
* Quantum Randomness
* Quantum-Safe Blockchain
* Quantum Threats to Blockchain
* Quantum-Resistant Consensus
* Other relevant areas discovered during research

Blockchain areas should be considered as described in Domain A.

---

# 3. Main Objective

Discover a technically meaningful, research-oriented and potentially novel problem within either:

Blockchain × AI

OR

Blockchain × Quantum

The final project should ideally have:

* a clearly defined problem
* existing literature
* a research gap
* a research question
* a testable hypothesis
* an experimental methodology
* measurable evaluation
* meaningful baselines
* realistic implementation requirements
* a potential technical contribution

---

# 4. Exploratory Approach

Do NOT select the final direction at the beginning.

The research should proceed through exploration.

Within each domain, investigate multiple possible directions before narrowing down.

For example:

Blockchain × AI

→ AI for Blockchain

→ Blockchain for AI

→ AI + Blockchain systems

→ different AI methods

→ different Blockchain components

→ identify research gaps

Similarly:

Blockchain × Quantum

→ Quantum for Blockchain

→ Blockchain for Quantum

→ Quantum-safe Blockchain

→ Blockchain infrastructure for Quantum systems

→ cryptography/security

→ algorithms/optimization

→ identify research gaps

Do not assume that every combination is meaningful.

Only pursue combinations where there is a real technical relationship or research problem.

---

# 5. Learning Requirement

This is a learning-oriented group project.

The group may not initially know all of the relevant technologies.

Therefore, before recommending a research direction, explain:

1. What the technology is
2. Why it exists
3. How it works
4. How it relates to Blockchain
5. Current applications
6. Current limitations
7. Existing research
8. Open problems
9. What the group would need to learn to work on it

The research process should help the group understand the field while discovering the problem.

---

# 6. Research Problem Classification

Classify candidate problems as:

* Established
* Active Research
* Partially Solved
* Emerging
* Underexplored
* Potentially Novel

Do not claim that a problem is novel simply because an initial search did not find an existing solution.

Potential novelty must be verified through broader literature searches.

---

# 7. Research Evidence

Prioritize:

* peer-reviewed papers
* major conferences
* academic journals
* arXiv/preprints where appropriate
* technical reports
* standards
* benchmark papers
* authoritative documentation

Prioritize recent research, especially 2024–2026, while including foundational research where necessary.

Never fabricate citations.

Clearly distinguish:

* established facts
* claims made by researchers
* interpretation
* hypothesis
* unresolved questions

---

# 8. Candidate Problem Requirements

A candidate problem should ideally have:

* specific technical problem
* clear research gap
* testable research question
* falsifiable hypothesis
* experimental methodology
* baseline methods
* evaluation metrics
* accessible data or reproducible environment
* realistic compute requirements
* realistic development timeline
* measurable contribution

Avoid projects that simply demonstrate that two technologies can be connected.

---

# 9. Group Constraints

This is a group project.

The group will update this document as the project develops.

Future updates may include:

* group member skills
* available compute
* cloud resources
* available hardware
* programming languages
* preferred technologies
* time available
* project deadline
* excluded approaches
* preferred research areas
* advisor feedback
* newly discovered constraints

Treat the latest version of this section as the source of truth.

---

# 10. Decision Process

The group will make the final project decision.

Do not prematurely select a winner.

When comparing directions, provide:

* evidence
* existing research
* limitations
* research gaps
* feasibility
* risks
* required knowledge
* expected experimental difficulty
* potential contribution

Do not give arbitrary numerical scores or rankings.

The purpose is to help the group make an informed decision.

---

# 11. Final Deliverable

After selecting a research problem, the project should eventually produce:

Research Problem
→ Literature Review
→ Research Gap
→ Research Question
→ Hypothesis
→ Methodology
→ Implementation
→ Experiments
→ Evaluation
→ Results
→ Analysis
→ Limitations
→ Contribution

The final project should produce evidence and analysis, not merely a technology demonstration.
